# Catchment-attribute lookup: effect on unseen rivers

Generated by `scripts/eval_attribute_lookup.py`. 80 Central European rivers (LaMAH-CE, random draw, seed 1), never seen by the submitted model (trained on US rivers only). Same inputs, same model; the only difference is whether missing catchment attributes are filled from `models/catchment_attributes.parquet`.

| | 1 day | 3 days | 7 days |
|---|---|---|---|
| NSE, median over rivers: without → **with** lookup | 0.714 → **0.724** | 0.262 → **0.350** | 0.142 → **0.167** |
| High-flow AUPRC: without → **with** lookup | 0.747 → **0.763** | 0.378 → **0.432** | 0.230 → **0.257** |
| Brier score, high flow (lower is better): without → **with** lookup | 0.028 → **0.024** | 0.048 → **0.041** | 0.057 → **0.048** |
| 10–90% range coverage (target 0.80): without → **with** lookup | 0.857 → **0.766** | 0.913 → **0.823** | 0.932 → **0.819** |
| Per-river NSE change: mean [95% CI], rivers better | +0.046 [+0.034, +0.059], better on 66/80 | +0.089 [+0.063, +0.124], better on 71/80 | +0.085 [+0.048, +0.139], better on 57/80 |
| High-flow days scored | 6,716 | 6,713 | 6,723 |

**Reading.** With real attributes the flood probabilities are better at every horizon (higher AUPRC, lower Brier score) and the 10–90% range moves from too wide to close to the 80% target. Flow forecasts improve too: the median over rivers rises at every horizon and most rivers get better, with the largest gains on rivers whose guessed attributes were far off. Adopted as the default in `predict.py` and on the website; `--no-attribute-lookup` turns it off, and a user's own attribute file always takes precedence.
