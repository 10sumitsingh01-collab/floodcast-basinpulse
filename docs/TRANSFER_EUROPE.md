# Zero-shot transfer to Central Europe

Models fitted on **239 US basins only** (CAMELS-US, 1981–2008), then applied without any retraining to **579 Central European basins** (LaMAH-CE: Austria, Germany, Switzerland), test period 2008-10 to 2014-09. Same inputs, attribute recipe and event definition (95th / 99th percentile of 1981–2008 flow). 95% CIs resample basins. Generated by `scripts/transfer_test.py`.
