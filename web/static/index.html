    HOW IT WORKS

    1. 01 · DATA

      Caravan / CAMELS-US: observed USGS flow, basin-averaged ERA5-Land weather, HydroATLAS catchment traits. 671 rivers, 1981–2014. Real observations only.

    2. 02 · NO PEEKING

      Rivers split first, then years. 60 rivers locked away. 15 automated leakage checks. Every choice made on validation, the test scored once.

    3. 03 · THE MODEL

      Gradient-boosted trees predict how much the river will change, not its level, so one model fits rivers a thousand times apart in size. Trained with gaps and late readings.

    4. 04 · HONEST RANGES

      Quantile forecasts with conformal calibration on rivers the trees never saw; flood probabilities calibrated the same way. 80% means 80%.

    Research benchmark entry for BASINSHIFT. Not a flood-warning service: never the sole basis for evacuation, emergency response or public warnings.
